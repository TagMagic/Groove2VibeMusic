GALLERY  

Groove2Vibe Music presents a series of collectible art & music greeting cards featuring original visual artwork, each paired with its own listening experience. Every card includes a QR code that opens a music video created specifically for that piece—extending the artwork through sound and motion. Each purchase also includes a
Certificate of Authenticity delivered by email

The music moves freely across styles, from ambient and meditative soundscapes to jazz, funk, R&B, rock, country rock, lo-fi, and beyond. Rather than focusing on genre, each piece is shaped by mood, rhythm, and atmosphere, allowing image and music to meet in their own way

These works are meant to be lived with, seen often, and listened to when the moment feels right
When Bunnies Fly

A soft moment suspended between earth &
imagination

When Bunnies Fly features a gentle winged bunny hovering above blooming tulips, surrounded by drifting butterflies—an image that invites curiosity, warmth, and a return to simple wonder. There’s a lightness here, a sense of pause, as if time slows just long enough to notice what’s unfolding

The accompanying piece, Sacred Flow (Psy Dub Meditation tuned to 232 Hz), moves with an easy, hypnotic rhythm—grounded yet spacious. Subtle pulses and atmospheric textures create a meditative current that supports relaxation while keeping a quiet sense of motion beneath the surface

Together, image and sound form a calm, playful meditation—one that encourages ease, imagination, and gentle presence whenever you choose to return to it

Forest Path Home

A quiet journey inward, guided by nature


Forest Path Home presents a winding forest path bathed in soft light—an invitation to slow down and reconnect with the natural world. The image carries a sense of return, as if the forest itself is offering a way back to balance, presence, and oneness

The accompanying piece, Forest Spirits Blessing, unfolds with the steady pulse of Native American–inspired drums and the open, breathing tones of flutes. The rhythm is grounding without urgency, while the melodies feel ceremonial and spacious, creating a gentle sense of movement through stillness

Together, image and sound form a meditative passage—one that encourages reflection, and the feeling of being quietly guided home

Whale Of A Time

A meeting of breath, water, and deep listening


Whale Of A Time captures the powerful grace of a humpback whale breaching the surface in front of Morro Rock, where ocean and shoreline meet in a moment of shared presence. There’s a sense of scale here—of life moving freely between worlds, rising briefly into view before returning to the depths

The accompanying piece, Underwater Dreaming, blends ambient lo-fi textures with ethereal tones and the natural resonance of real whale songs. Soft rhythms drift beneath the surface while spacious layers unfold slowly, creating a listening experience that feels fluid, weightless, and deeply calming

Together, image and sound invite you into an underwater reverie—one that encourages stillness, wonder, and a gentle sense of connection to something far larger than the moment itself

Wild & Free

Motion guided by instinct, strength carried with ease


Wild & Free features a powerful white horse—part myth, part living force—galloping through ocean surf, embodying freedom without resistance and grace without restraint. There’s confidence in the movement, but also calm, as if the horse knows exactly where it belongs

The accompanying song, I Live For My Comfort, is an original country rock piece that reflects living on your own terms—choosing balance, independence, and authenticity over expectation. With steady rhythm and an open melodic feel, the music carries a sense of forward motion that feels natural rather than forced

Together, image and sound express freedom as a state of being—strong, self-directed, and unhurried... where movement flows from intention, and ease is found in staying true to oneself

ABOUT

Music & Art have been part of my life for as long as I can remember. Over the decades, I’ve explored countless styles, sounds, and creative paths—not as a pursuit of labels, but as a way of understanding mood, movement, and meaning

What continues to draw me back is the act of shaping a sound, forming an image, and noticing how they affect the way a moment feels. Some pieces arrive quietly, others with rhythm and energy, but all of them begin from the same place: curiosity, emotion, and the desire to make something that resonates

I create music and visual art as a single conversation... lyrics, textures, colors, and rhythm inform one another, often blurring the line between what is seen and what is heard. Each piece reflects a feeling I’ve experienced... calm, motion, reflection, release, and the quiet satisfaction of giving that feeling form

Working from my home studio in San Luis Obispo,
these pieces are shared in the hope they find their place with others...
creating moments of connection, calm, and groove along the way

Thank you for being here,

Chuck Siler
